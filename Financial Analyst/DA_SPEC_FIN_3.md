# Graded task description

You are a financial analyst working in a startup company that produces gadgets for smart homes. The company had 9 employees at the beginning of 2016 and 60 at the end of 2017. Your company's COO has come up with 2 development scenarios and asked you to evaluate which one company should go for.

The first investment opportunity is to build an extension of a factory building and increase manufacturing activities. The second scenario suggests building a digital product and investing in marketing. Here are more details about the projects you need to consider:

### Assumptions for the first scenario:

- **Project**: to build a factory, including buildings and machine tools required for production.
- **Increase in sales**: It is planned that after the implementation of the project, the sales volumes will increase by 50% from the current ones and should continue to increase by 10% annually.
- **Cost**: An additional 10 people with an average salary of the company will be needed to service the new production capacity.
- **Costs**: Marketing budgets would need to be further adjusted to maintain projected sales growth.
- **Duration of the project**: 2 years.

### Assumptions for the second scenario:

- **Project**: will carry out active marketing activities and develop a digital product.
- **Increase in sales**: Sales are expected to grow by 30% during the campaign every year. In subsequent periods, growth is expected to fall to 15%.
- **Cost**: The provision and development of services will require the hiring of 10 people with 2x the average salary of the company, starting in the first year.
- **Costs**: After the campaign ends, advertising budgets are expected to fall to € 1 million per year.
- **Duration of the project**: 2 years.

The company would allocate 5.4 mln. to the first scenario and 8 mln. to the second one. The money for investment will be collected from own funds, credit institutions, and private investors. The projected cost of the projects, as well as investment, is presented in the table below.

You have to use this information while analyzing development scenarios, and your goal is to decide which scenario the company should invest in, if any. Your suggestion has to be based on the investment project comparisons by using financial metrics like:

- Opportunity costs
- Payback period
- IRR(internal return rate)
- NPV(net present value)
- ROI(return on investment)

To calculate those metrics, you should use the given assumptions, investment allocation table, and company financial statements. However, the information given is not sufficient, and you will need to make some assumptions yourself, like what the average salary is (you have just the total salary cost per year and do not know the employee number each month), inflation rate, discount norm, how many years to use in evaluation, etc.

# Evaluation criteria:

- Correctly estimated NPV

- Investment projects are compared considering estimated financial indicators
- Assumptions made supported with background information (for example, the inflation rate is taken from macroeconomic data)
- The findings are clearly presented, based on calculations and data-driven conclusions are made
