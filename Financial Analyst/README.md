# Investment Scenario Financial Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes two potential development scenarios for a startup company producing **smart home gadgets**. The goal is to evaluate which investment option the company should pursue based on financial analysis and capital budgeting techniques.

The company experienced rapid growth, expanding from 9 employees in 2016 to 60 employees in 2017, and is now considering two strategic investment opportunities:

1.  **Scenario 1 – Factory Expansion**
    * Building an extension of the production facility to increase manufacturing capacity.
2.  **Scenario 2 – Digital Product Development**
    * Developing a digital product and investing heavily in marketing activities.

The purpose of this analysis is to compare these two investment opportunities using financial metrics and determine which scenario provides the best financial outcome.

---

## Financial Evaluation Methods
To evaluate the projects, several capital budgeting metrics were used:

* **Net Present Value (NPV)**
* **Internal Rate of Return (IRR)**
* **Payback Period**
* **Return on Investment (ROI)**
* **Opportunity Cost**

These metrics help determine the profitability, risk, and efficiency of each investment.

---

## Data Sources
The analysis is based on the following information provided in the task:

* Company financial statements (2016–2017)
* Project investment tables
* Scenario assumptions
* Salary and operating cost data
* Loan and financing conditions

Financial projections were built using these datasets combined with reasonable assumptions where information was missing.

---

## Key Assumptions
Since not all required data was provided, several assumptions were made to complete the financial analysis.

### Average Salary
The company did not provide a clear average salary. Therefore, it was estimated using total wage expenses from the financial statements.
* **Estimated Salary:** €39,666 per employee per year.
* *Calculation:* Total wage costs divided by the estimated number of employees.

### Inflation Rate
* **Assumed Rate:** 2%
* This value is consistent with the European Central Bank's long-term inflation target. Inflation was included to represent realistic long-term economic conditions.

### Discount Rate
* **Assumed Rate:** 10%
* This rate approximates the **Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)**. It reflects a 3% bank loan interest rate, 5% private investment return, and an equity risk premium typical for high-risk startups.

### Evaluation Period
* **Duration:** 10 years
* This time horizon captures the long-term effects of the investment and allows sufficient time for projects to generate returns.

### Marketing & Profit Margins
* **Scenario 1 Marketing:** 5% of incremental revenue.
* **Scenario 2 Marketing:** €1,000,000 per year (post-campaign).
* **Profit Margin (Scenario 1):** Increased to **15%** (from historical 8.28%) to reflect economies of scale and improved operational efficiency.

---

## Scenario Descriptions

### Scenario 1 – Factory Expansion
The first investment scenario involves expanding the production facility and purchasing additional machinery.
* **Project Duration:** 2 years (2018–2019)
* **Total Investment:** €5,418,273
* **Sales Increase:** 50% after completion, with 10% annual growth thereafter.
* **Staffing:** +10 additional employees.

### Scenario 2 – Digital Product Development
The second scenario focuses on developing a digital product and aggressive marketing.
* **Total Investment:** €8,000,000
* **Sales Growth:** 30% annually during campaign, 15% thereafter.
* **Staffing:** +10 additional employees at 2× the average salary.
* **Marketing Cost:** €1,000,000 fixed per year.

---

## Financial Metrics Results

| Metric | Scenario 1 | Scenario 2 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **NPV** | -$1,309,275.00 | -€4,261,785.73 |
| **IRR** | -3% | 35% |
| **Payback Period** | 3 years | 3 years |
| **ROI** | -7% | 79% |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Opportunity Cost:** If Scenario 1 is selected, the opportunity cost equals the NPV of Scenario 2. This represents the potential value lost by not selecting the alternative.

---

## Final Recommendation
Scenario 1 generates negative NPV and IRR below the discount rate, indicating that the factory expansion project is not financially viable under current assumptions.				
				
Scenario 2 generates positive NPV (€4.26M) and IRR of 35%, significantly exceeding the 10% discount rate.				
				
Scenario 2 — the digital product and marketing strategy — is the financially preferable investment option.
