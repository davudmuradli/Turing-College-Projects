Your PM would like to see the users' duration from first arriving on the website on any given day until their first purchase on that same day. Your final result should display the daily progression of duration.

- Create a presentation centered around the dynamic daily duration.
- See whether you can apply one or two techniques from the module's course material to enhance your presentation on this subject.
